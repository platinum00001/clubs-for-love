Cape Cod

Used or new, golf clubs on Cape Cod become a first bag for someone in recovery.

Give the set in the garage — used or new — or give a round at your course. Shops, simulators, and neighbors all count. No login to give.

How it works

Four quiet steps.

01

Photo

Snap the clubs, shoes, or rain gear from the porch.

02

Pickup or drop

We come to you, or you leave a bag in Falmouth or Mashpee.

03

We match or auction

A first bag goes out. Extra sets may be auctioned. Gifted rounds get someone on the tee.

04

Someone tees off

Auction money pays green fees, lessons, and recovery golf days.
